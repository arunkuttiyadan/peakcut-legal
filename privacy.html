Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Shambunni (“I,” “me,” or “the operator”) handles information when using PeakCut, a personal tool for preparing video clips and posting them to the operator’s TikTok account.

1. Information used

When the operator connects TikTok, PeakCut may use the authorization information and account details TikTok makes available for the enabled features. PeakCut also handles the video clips and captions the operator selects for upload. TikTok may collect and process information under its own privacy policy.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to authorize the operator’s TikTok account and to prepare or upload the operator’s selected content. PeakCut is a personal tool and is not intended to collect information from the public.

3. Sharing

I do not sell personal information. PeakCut does not share information with third parties for their independent marketing. Information necessary to authorize or upload content is transmitted to TikTok, which handles it under TikTok’s own terms and privacy policy.

4. Storage and security

Information is kept only as needed to use the app’s features. The operator takes reasonable steps to protect access to the app and account credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Your choices and revoking access

The operator can stop using PeakCut and revoke its access to TikTok at any time through TikTok’s account settings or connected-app controls. The operator may also contact me at the address below with privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by PeakCut. Revoking access does not remove content already posted to TikTok; manage or delete that content through TikTok.

6. Children

PeakCut is a personal tool and is not directed to children. It is not intended for use by anyone who is not permitted to use TikTok under TikTok’s age requirements.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

8. Contact
